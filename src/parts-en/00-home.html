What is this?

Who are the Tamils?

A people of more than eighty million. Most live in Tamil Nadu in southern India and in Sri Lanka, with communities in Malaysia, Singapore and around the world. Their language, Tamil, has been written for over two thousand years and is among the oldest languages still spoken today.
